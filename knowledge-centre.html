ITR Filing Deadline for Businesses (Non-Audit)

Please be advised that the due date for filing Income Tax Returns (ITR-3, ITR-4) for individuals and HUFs having business or professional income (who are not liable for a tax audit) for the Financial Year 2025-26 is approaching.
